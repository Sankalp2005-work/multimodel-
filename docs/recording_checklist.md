# Recording Checklist

## Setup
- [ ] Use the same microphone for all takes (do not switch between laptop and headset midway).
- [ ] Record in a quiet room with minimal background noise.
- [ ] Maintain a consistent distance from the microphone (about 6-8 inches).
- [ ] Keep phone/stopwatch visible for timing pauses but silent.

## Recording the Ideal Takes
- [ ] Read the transcript at a natural, consistent pace.
- [ ] Ensure clear articulation of consonants.
- [ ] Record 2 "ideal" takes per transcript.
- [ ] Verify SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio) is ≥ 25 dB and there is no clipping.

## Recording the Flawed Takes (Applied ONLY to the target sentence)
- [ ] **Rushed Pace:** Race through the designated target sentence (~1.4x to 1.8x speed) but remain intelligible.
- [ ] **Dead Pause:** Stop mid-sentence at a natural break for 1-2.5 seconds.
- [ ] **Flat Pitch:** Use a monotone, robotic delivery on the target sentence. Do not whisper.
- [ ] **Mumbled Clarity:** Slur consonants and soften articulation on the target sentence. Do not move away from the mic.
